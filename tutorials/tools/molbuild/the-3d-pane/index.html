The 3D pane¶

What it does¶

The pane on the right, titled 3D PREVIEW, shows the three-dimensional structure that goes with your drawing: balls and sticks. It is a viewer only. You cannot draw, select or pick atoms in it; you draw in the 2D canvas on the left, and the pane follows.

A small label next to the title says where the structure comes from and whether it is up to date:

Label Meaning
OPTIMIZED the result of Optimise
IMPORTED read from a file (Load a structure)
EMBED a quick preview built from the drawing, not optimised
MOVED the result of a fragment move

Beside it, ✓ current means the structure belongs to the drawing as it is now, and ⚠ stale means the drawing has been edited since.

How¶

  1. Draw a molecule. About half a second after your last edit, a preview appears in the pane (EMBED). The pane already holds the preview of the ethanol that MolBuild shows when it opens. If you click ✕ Clear, the pane keeps the last structure, marked ⚠ stale, until you draw something again. The text No 3D geometry yet and Run ⚡ Optimise or ⬆ Load a 3D file belongs to a pane that has never held a structure, which you will normally not see.
  2. Turn the structure: drag with the left mouse button (or one finger). Drag with the right or middle button to pan. Scroll the wheel to zoom. On a touch screen, one finger turns the structure, and two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.
  3. Click ⟲ Reset at the right end of the pane's header to bring back the first orientation, with the whole molecule in view.
  4. Click ⚡ Optimise to replace the preview by an optimised structure, or ⬆ Load to show a structure from a file.

Tips and pitfalls¶

The preview is not the optimised structure

EMBED is a fast guess at the geometry. Use it to see that the drawing makes sense, and run ⚡ Optimise before you export.

What an edit does to the pane

After you edit the drawing, the pane briefly shows ⚠ stale, and then it is ✓ current again. What it shows depends on where the structure came from. An EMBED preview is built again from the new drawing. A structure you loaded or optimised keeps its coordinates for the atoms that were already there, and its label (IMPORTED or OPTIMIZED); only the atoms you added are placed, next to their neighbours.

↻ Rebuild 3D from the drawing

When the pane holds coordinates read from a file, or the result of ⚡ Optimise (on a drawn molecule too), a button ↻ Rebuild 3D from the drawing appears in the header. It throws those coordinates away and builds a new structure from the drawing. The first click asks you to confirm; click again within four seconds, or nothing changes.

Hiding the pane

The handle at the right edge of the canvas hides and shows the pane, which the 2D canvas then fills. The pane keeps drawing while it is hidden. See Side panels.

Hydrogens in the 2D drawing

The H button in the toolbar affects the 2D drawing only; the 3D pane does not follow it.
